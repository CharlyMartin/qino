A good slug is short, descriptive, and stable. Two of those are easy. Stability is where most slugs fail.

| Slug                                | Verdict |
|-------------------------------------|---------|
| `2024-launching-our-new-platform`   | Bad — date is irrelevant in 2027 |
| `business/launching-platform`       | Bad — category will change |
| `launching-our-new-platform`        | OK   |
| `our-platform`                      | Better — short and stable |

Avoid words you'll regret: "new", "latest", "today", "v2". They're true for about a week.

The unsexy truth: slugs are about *future you*. Five years from now, you want them to still describe the post and still resolve to the same URL.
