The first time you switch a writer from a WYSIWYG editor to Markdown, they panic. The second time, they ask why nobody told them sooner.

WYSIWYG editors hide structure behind appearance. You see bold text but not whether it's a `<strong>`, an `<h3>`, or a span with inline styles. That ambiguity is fine until you need to do anything _with_ the content — translate it, restyle it, syndicate it.

## What plain text gives you

- **Predictable structure.** A heading is a heading.
- **Diffable history.** Git can tell you exactly what changed.
- **Tool-agnostic editing.** Vim, VS Code, iA Writer, a text box on your phone.

The trade-off is real — there's a learning curve — but it's a one-time cost in exchange for years of leverage.
