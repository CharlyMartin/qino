A URL is a promise. Once it's published, breaking it costs you backlinks, search ranking, and the trust of anyone who bookmarked it. Slugs are the load-bearing part of that promise.

A few rules of thumb:

1. Pick the slug carefully on day one — it's much harder to change later.
2. Don't put dates in slugs unless you genuinely want the date in the URL.
3. Don't put categories in slugs. Categories change; URLs shouldn't.
4. Keep them short, lowercase, and kebab-case.

If you ever *do* need to rename a slug, treat it like a database migration: add a redirect, deploy, then change the slug. Never the other way around.
