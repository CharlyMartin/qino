I used a popular headless CMS for three years. The product was good. The vendor lock-in was the problem.

Every project shipped with a runtime dependency on someone else's API. When the API changed, my site changed. When the pricing model changed, my budget changed. When the company pivoted, my migration plan started forming.

> The cost of leaving a CMS isn't the cost of the new one — it's the cost of *re-modeling all your content* in the new one's primitives.

Flat files don't have that problem. They're already the lowest common denominator. Migrating between flat-file tools is a folder rename.

That alone isn't a reason to switch. But combined with faster local dev, free version control, and no API rate limits — it adds up.
