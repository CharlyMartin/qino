Flat-file CMSes have been around forever — but the version we've had until now was *for developers*. The next version has to be for everyone else, too.

A few bets on where this is going:

1. **Editorial UIs that hide Git completely.** Writers shouldn't know they're committing.
2. **Schemas as the contract.** Validated frontmatter, generated types, end-to-end safety.
3. **Build steps that feel instant.** Sub-second preview from edit to render.
4. **Cloud-readable repos.** A `qino-lock.json` that lets a hosted dashboard understand the project without running it.
5. **Better localization.** The current state of the art is "duplicate the file". We can do better.

> The promise of flat-file CMS isn't "files instead of a database". It's "the durability of files, with the polish of a SaaS".

That's the gap worth closing. Files were always the right storage layer. The product around them is what's been missing.
