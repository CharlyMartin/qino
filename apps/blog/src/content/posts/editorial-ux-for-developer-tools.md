Developer tools tend to optimize for developers — that's the whole point. But content tools sit at a strange intersection: developers build them, *non-developers* use them daily.

The result is usually a tool that's powerful and unloved.

A few things that help:

- **Don't show YAML to writers.** Render it as a form. Let them never see a colon.
- **Inline previews.** Side-by-side beats tab-switching, every time.
- **Progressive disclosure.** Hide tags, drafts, and SEO behind a "more" button. Most posts don't need them visible by default.
- **Save without thinking.** Auto-save on blur. Writers shouldn't have to remember a keyboard shortcut to not lose work.

The real test: can your mom publish a post? If the answer is no, you've got more UX work to do.
