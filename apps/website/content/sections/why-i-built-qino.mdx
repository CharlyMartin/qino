---
title: Why qino, and why now?
---

In the AI era, it makes less and less sense to keep your content apart from your code, for one simple reason: <Underline>agents are only as good as the data you give them</Underline>.

Agents already read your code, grep it, edit it, and open PRs, without asking permission. Your content deserves the same. Behind someone else's API, it adds friction and limits what your agents can do.

As Lee Robinson [wrote](https://leerob.com/agents) after moving Cursor's site from a CMS to Markdown: _"With AI and coding agents, the cost of an abstraction has never been higher."_

Markdown is a fantastic format for content, and LLMs love it. But on its own, it has two problems:

- **No guardrails.** Without structure, content quickly becomes a mess.
- **No editor.** Non-developers can't publish without a developer.

qino solves both. Define your content with Zod schemas and query it with end-to-end types, in any framework. `qino check` validates every edit your agents make, and the [cloud editor](/cloud) lets everyone else publish without touching code.

With qino, <Underline>you own your content layer</Underline> without giving up the convenience of a cloud CMS.
